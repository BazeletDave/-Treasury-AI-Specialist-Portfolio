Portfolio Overview

This portfolio supports my application for the IT Specialist Artificial Intelligence role with the U.S. Department of the Treasury, Common Services Center, Office of the Deputy Administrator for Technology Services Operations.

The work focuses on practical AI implementation, responsible governance, management of algorithmic systems, human decision authority, organizational design, and the translation of emerging AI capabilities into operational environments.

Governed AI Design Principles

01

Human Accountability

Final responsibility for consequential decisions remains identifiable and reviewable.

02

Authorization Boundaries

AI systems should operate within clearly defined authority, permissions, and escalation rules.

03

Traceability

Important system actions and recommendations should be documented and auditable where operationally appropriate.

04

Security by Design

Security, access control, data governance, and operational risk should be considered throughout the AI lifecycle.

05

Institutional Alignment

AI systems should support organizational missions and policies rather than create unaccountable parallel decision structures.
