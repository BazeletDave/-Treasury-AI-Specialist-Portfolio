## Case template

Each case record should include: ID, question, date and version, institution and function, capability demonstrated, data classification, access and authorization boundary, human decision authority, observed evidence, sources and access dates, limitations, threat model, controls, validation outcomes, and next review date.

**Boundary:** Neither exercise establishes access to Treasury production infrastructure or official government endorsement. Scenario interviews in the TTB project brief are illustrative material and must not be represented as independently conducted federal interviews.
