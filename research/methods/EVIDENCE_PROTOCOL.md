# Evidence, Provenance and Research Integrity Protocol

1. Prefer primary sources: statutes, regulations, agency guidance, original technical documentation, reproducible code, and peer reviewed research.
2. Capture source title, issuer, URL or persistent identifier, publication date, retrieval date, jurisdiction, and relevant version.
3. Label statements as **observed**, **documented by source**, **inferred**, **hypothesis**, or **unverified**.
4. Distinguish government policy, proposed policy, advisory guidance, and commentary.
5. Record system boundaries and distinguish a public prototype from an authorized agency integration.
6. Never infer compromise, vulnerability exploitation, or deployment from a general federal warning.
7. For experiments record data provenance, configuration, test set, metrics, limitations, and reproducibility instructions.
8. Track corrections and superseded findings without silently rewriting historical records.
9. Respect confidentiality, intellectual property, licensing, and personal data restrictions.
10. Require human editorial review before publication of high consequence conclusions.
