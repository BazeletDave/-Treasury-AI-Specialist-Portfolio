# Treasury AI Specialist Portfolio

## Overview

This repository supports my portfolio submission for the IT Specialist (Artificial Intelligence) role with the U.S. Department of the Treasury, Common Services Center, Office of the Deputy Administrator for Technology Services Operations.

The portfolio demonstrates applied artificial intelligence, AI governance, algorithmic management, responsible system design, technical communication, and human oversight concepts relevant to public sector technology operations.

Live portfolio:

## Project Objectives

The project demonstrates the ability to:

* Design and communicate an applied AI solution
* Translate AI concepts into operational and management requirements
* Incorporate governance, accountability, and human oversight
* Document technical assumptions and system architecture
* Present AI capabilities through a working deployed application
* Connect AI implementation with public sector mission requirements

## Technical Approach

The portfolio uses a web based application architecture designed to present AI projects, research, governance concepts, and implementation examples in a clear and accessible format.

The approach emphasizes:

* Human oversight of AI supported decisions
* Transparent system design
* Responsible use of automated and algorithmic systems
* Documentation and traceability
* Governance throughout the AI lifecycle
* Separation of AI recommendations from final human authority

## AI Governance Framework

The portfolio reflects a governance first approach to artificial intelligence.

Key design principles include:

1. Human accountability remains identifiable.
2. AI recommendations should be reviewable.
3. Automated decisions should be traceable where practical.
4. Authorization boundaries should be explicit.
5. Sensitive systems require appropriate controls.
6. AI systems should support institutional objectives rather than replace institutional accountability.

## Assumptions

This portfolio is a demonstration environment.

It does not connect to Treasury production systems, government databases, controlled information, or operational federal infrastructure.

Examples are designed to demonstrate architecture, reasoning, governance, management, and implementation concepts rather than represent deployed Treasury systems.

## Security and Responsible AI

The project follows several core principles:

* No production Treasury data is used.
* No classified or controlled government information is included.
* Demonstrations use conceptual or simulated scenarios.
* Human review remains central to consequential decision making.
* Security, authorization, auditability, and governance are treated as core design requirements.
